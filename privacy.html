Privacy Policy

App: Bot Battle Poster · Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Summary

Bot Battle Poster is a personal tool that uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It does not collect data from the public, does not track viewers, and does not sell or share personal information.

2. Information the app uses

  • Basic TikTok profile info (such as display name and account ID), to confirm which account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok when you approve the app, so it can upload videos on your behalf. These are stored by the connection service Composio and are not shared with anyone else.
  • The videos and captions you choose to upload.

The app does not access your followers, messages, contacts, likes, or other users' data.

3. How it is used

Only to upload and publish your own videos to your account when you ask it to. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

4. Sharing

Information is shared only with TikTok (to perform the upload) and with Composio (which securely stores the access tokens). It is not sold, rented, or given to anyone else.

5. Retention and deletion

Access tokens are kept only while the app is connected. Disconnecting the app in TikTok's settings, or revoking it in Composio, removes its access. To have any remaining data deleted, email the contact below.

6. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
